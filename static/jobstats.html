How the numbers are computed
  • Per-job GPU columns are the mean over that job's GPUs that report the field. A dash means no value, never zero. GPU % is NVML utilization; SM, TC (tensor) and DRAM are GPM fractions shown as percent.
  • GPU-h is allocated GPUs × elapsed, for every job. Metrics come only from jobs with a summary (ok).
  • Totals and group means are weighted by GPU-seconds: Σ(elapsed × value × GPUs with the field) / Σ(elapsed × GPUs with the field). Spread (p10, median, p90) is over per-job values, unweighted, with 3 or more jobs.
  • CPU eff is Σ CPU-seconds used / Σ CPU-seconds allocated. Memory columns are peaks: totals and groups show the max (and the median in groups) of per-job peak %, never an average. FP16 / FP64 is the share of jobs where nonzero activity was seen.
  • Coverage is GPU-seconds with a GPU % value over allocated GPU-seconds, for jobs with a summary.
  • Summaries store time means without sample counts, so a mean assumes the sampled period represents the run.
  • Summaries exist for jobs that ended after 29 Oct 19:10 UTC. not written marks a job that ran and ended after that, more than 10 min ago, without one.
